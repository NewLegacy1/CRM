# VSL shoot + edit brief

One session. You on camera. Simple graphics. CapCut after.

---

## How it should look

Think Hormozi talking-head, not a brand film.

- You, chest-up, looking at the lens
- Clean wall or desk. One lamp or window in front of your face
- Solid shirt
- **Captions on the whole time** (trades watch on mute in the truck)
- Big words on screen only when you hit a line that matters — not bouncing stickers the whole video
- Cut to a screen recording for Jay / ShowRoom / a Maps search. Then back to your face.

Do not: green screen, stock footage of handshakes, auto-zoom every sentence, royalty-free “inspiring” music under your voice.

---

## What you film (raw)

**Camera:** iPhone, 1080p, back camera if you can lock it on a stand. Horizontal for VSL A/B (thank-you page). After the A take, do a 15-second vertical of the hook + “text READY” for ads later.

**Audio:** Wired lav or AirPods. If the room echoes, closet + blanket. Bad audio kills the video.

**Takes:**
1. VSL A all the way through (2–3 full takes). Keep the one where you sound like yourself.
2. VSL B (one good take).
3. **B-roll, screen record (QuickTime or iPhone):**
   - Google search: `plumber near me` / `furnace repair near me` — map pack
   - A thin GMB (few photos, few reviews) vs a strong one
   - Jay That Drain Guy listing (reviews)
   - ShowRoom site or GMB
4. Optional: 3 seconds of you holding the phone as if you just texted READY.

## What goes on screen (add in CapCut — not while you film)

| When | On screen | Motion |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Whole video | Captions, white, bottom third, fat stroke so it reads on a phone | None. Just on. |
| “Three names” | **3 NAMES** | Pop on, hold 1.5s, cut |
| Invisible / untrusted / slow | three words, one at a time | Simple pop |
| Booked Jobs Launch | **BOOKED JOBS** | Hold |
| The three parts (profile / page / text-back) | 1 / 2 / 3 labels | Cut, don’t spin |
| Jay / ShowRoom | screenshot + “96+ reviews” / “135+ reviews” | Hard cut to screen, hold 4–6s |
| $2,997 | **$2,997 · 14 DAYS** | Once, when you say it |
| End of **VSL B only** | **Text READY to 905-975-4877** | Hold until B ends. Not on VSL A. |

That’s the “animation.” No logo sting, no particle trails.

---

## Edit stack (after the raw file)

1. **CapCut** (desktop) — cut ums, drop B-roll, captions, the 8 text cards above.
2. Caption style: one size, one font (Montserrat / Inter / default CapCut bold). Not three fonts.
3. Color: slight contrast, don’t orange-crush your face.
4. Export 1080p H.264.
5. Host: unlisted YouTube or Mux. Paste IDs into `NEXT_PUBLIC_VSL_A_ID` / `_B_ID`.

If you hate captions in CapCut: record with AutoCap or upload to YouTube and download the `.srt`, then drop it in.

**Time:** 2–3 hours edit for A, 30–45 min for B.

---

## Player on the thank-you page

Plain embed. Autoplay **off** (phones block it and it feels spammy). Big play button. No Viddalyzer / extra player week 1.

---

## Checklist before you call it done

- [ ] You can hear every word on a phone speaker
- [ ] Captions match what you said
- [ ] READY + number is on **VSL B only**, readable without sound
- [ ] VSL A ends on Book 15 min — no text-me
- [ ] No price on any **ad** cut. Price can stay in VSL A/B spoken line
- [ ] VSL B does not re-explain the whole offer
