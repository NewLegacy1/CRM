### SAY — the problem

A homeowner has a problem. Furnace. Burst pipe. Panel. Roof. They search.

Google shows three names. They call the first one that looks open, has reviews, and picks up.

If you’re not in that three, you don’t exist.  
If you are, and your profile looks empty, they skip you.  
If they call and it’s voicemail, they call the next one. They don’t leave a message.

Three leaks: they can’t find you, they don’t trust you, or you don’t pick up.

You’re not losing because the work is bad. You’re losing because the door they walk through is Google.
