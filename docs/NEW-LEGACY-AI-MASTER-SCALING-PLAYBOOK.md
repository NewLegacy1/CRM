## 1. Executive Summary

**Company:** New Legacy AI · Hamilton/GTA · newlegacyai.ca  
**Positioning:** Booked jobs for local service businesses via website, Google Business Profile, and follow-up systems.  
**Current capacity:** Can deliver **3 Launch projects/month** without burning out.  
**Stage:** Late **Stage 2 (Advertise)** → early **Stage 3 (Stabilize)** on the [$100M Scaling Roadmap](https://www.acquisition.com/roadmap).

**Strategic priorities (next 90 days):**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Focus** | One avatar, one front-end offer, one primary paid channel (Meta) |
| **Price** | Move from $1,700 commodity → **$2,997 Launch + $497–997/mo Grow** |
| **Acquire** | Meta ads → landing page → VSL → book call (Rule of 100: $100/day) |
| **Convert** | Pre-call VSL + 6-day email sequence + speed-to-lead (<5 min) |
| **Retain** | Grow tier, ascension to Scale ($5k+ CRM), referral credits |
| **Team** | VA first ($550–750/mo), commission cold callers, no salaries until MRR covers VA |

**North star:** *"We help local service businesses in Hamilton/GTA get more booked jobs from Google and their website — without hiring admin."*

## 4. Scaling Phases & Budget

### Phase A — Bootstrap (Month 1–2) · $0–400/mo labor

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Apify, tools | ~$100/mo |
| Commission cold caller | $0 base |
| **Target:** 2 Launch/mo @ $2,997 = **~$6,000/mo** |

### Phase B — Stabilize (Month 3–4) · $800–1,400/mo labor

| Hire | Cost | Role |
|------|------|------|
| Full-time VA (Philippines) | $550–750/mo | CRM, GMB, onboarding chase |
| Web contractor | $300–600/project | Template sites |
| Cold caller | Commission | 100 dials/day |
| **Target:** 3 Launch + 2 Grow = **~$10,000/mo** |
| **Launch COGS:** ~$480–510 → **~83% gross margin** |

### Phase C — Scale (Month 5–8) · $2,500–4,000/mo labor

| Hire | Cost |
|------|------|
| VA (keep) | $600–800 |
| Account manager | $400–800 or 5% MRR |
| Media buyer | $500/mo + 10% ad spend |
| Closer (commission) | 10% collected |
| **Target:** 4 Launch + 6 Grow + 1 Scale/qtr = **$15–20k/mo** |

**Break-even:** 1 Launch sale funds VA for ~1 month. 2 Grow clients fund VA indefinitely.

## 5. Hiring: VAs, Platforms & Pay Structure

### Where to hire (low capital)

| Role | Platform | Rate |
|------|----------|------|
| VA / admin / GMB | [OnlineJobs.ph](https://www.onlinejobs.ph) | $550–750 USD/mo FT |
| Cold caller | OnlineJobs.ph, Upwork | Commission-only |
| Web dev | OnlineJobs.ph, Upwork, Contra | $300–800/site |
| Media buyer | Upwork, local | $500/mo + % spend |
| Canadian closer | LinkedIn, network | Commission-only |

**Rule:** No salaries until **Grow MRR ≥ VA cost** (2 × $497 = $994/mo).

### Commission structure

**Cold caller / setter**

| Event | Pay (CAD) |
|-------|-----------|
| Qualified meeting booked | $40–75 |
| Meeting showed | +$25–50 |
| Deal closed (optional) | 3–5% collected |

**Qualification checklist:** Local trade, Hamilton/GTA, decision-maker, bad/missing web or GMB, can pay $2,500+ in 14 days.

**Closer:** 10% of collected (Launch ~$300, Scale 8–12% tiered).

**Account manager:** $50/active Grow client/mo OR 5% MRR; $75 onboarding bonus per on-time Launch.

**VA KPIs:** Assets collected <48hr, GMB live <7 days, zero missed follow-ups.

## 7. Meta Ads Strategy (2026)

### How the algorithm works (Andromeda + Advantage+)

- **Creative = targeting.** Meta reads hooks, visuals, and copy semantically — "If you own a plumbing business in Ontario" finds plumbers.  
  Sources: [Meta Andromeda official](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/the-creative-advantage-unlocking-the-power-of-diversification-with-meta-andromeda), [Chatterbuzz guide](https://www.chatterbuzzmedia.com/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-targeting/), [Hormozi 2026 strategy](https://sozai.app/transcript/alex-hormozi-facebook-ads-strategy-2026/).

- **Creative diversity > audience hacks.** 10–15 *conceptually distinct* creatives per campaign. Headline swaps alone get collapsed into one "Entity ID."  
  Source: [Meta creative diversification](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/demystifying-creative-diversification).

- **Broad geo + Advantage+ Audience.** Tight radius (Hamilton/GTA); leave interests broad.  
  Source: [Advantage+ for services](https://aiadvantageagency.com/what-is-meta-advantage-plus/).

- **Learning phase:** ~50 conversion events before stable. Avoid >20% budget swings for 7–10 days.

- **CAPI + Pixel:** Send Lead, Schedule, and Purchase events from CRM — not just form fills.

### Hormozi Meta tactics

| Tactic | Application |
|--------|-------------|
| **Horizontal scaling** | 20–50 micro-ads at $50–100/day each, one hook per trade/avatar |
| **Hook–Meat–CTA** | 50 hooks × 3–5 bodies × 1–3 CTAs |
| **Persona callouts** | "If you're a Hamilton HVAC owner still getting leads from word-of-mouth only…" |
| **Rule of 100** | Start **$100 CAD/day** for 100 days = ~$10k test budget |
| **Qualified > volume** | Same CAC, 8× customer value when ads speak to real owners |

Sources: [Horizontal scaling analysis](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/vishalsha_i-reverse-engineered-alex-hormozis-200m-activity-7424552225915592704-OUKV), [Hook-Meat-CTA](https://usegavel.com/alex-hormozi/hook-meat-cta), [Qualified customers](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alexhormozi_alexhormozi-acquisition-sales-activity-7201984205747089410-y4Or).

### Lead forms vs landing page (for $3k offer)

| Metric | Lead form | Landing page + VSL |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| CPL (typical) | ~$30 CAD | ~$55–65 CAD |
| SQL rate | ~8–15% | ~27–45% |
| **Cost per client** | ~$1,300+ | **~$400–650** |

**Recommendation:** Cold traffic → **landing page** (trust + qualification). Retargeting → testimonials + VSL.

Sources: [Instrumnt comparison](https://instrumnt.com/blog/lead-ads-vs-landing-page-scenario), [AdLibrary service playbook](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ads-for-service-based-business).

### Canadian benchmarks

| Metric | Range (CAD) |
|--------|-------------|
| CPL (service SMB) | $20–90 |
| Home services CPL | $25–45 |
| Min monthly spend | $800–1,500 |
| Target CPQL | **< $150** |
| Target cost per $3k client | **< $650** (4.6× ROAS) |

Sources: [GrowthLab CA](https://www.growthlabhq.ca/en/blog/facebook-ads-services), [WordStream 2025](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2025).

### Campaign structure

| Layer | Budget % | Creative |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Prospecting | 60–70% | 10–20 static hooks per trade |
| Retargeting (site, 25%+ video) | 15–25% | Case studies, VSL clip, urgency |
| Testing winners | 10–15% | New hooks, lookalikes from closed clients |

**Objective:** Leads → landing page (not Traffic). Optimize for **Schedule** or qualified Lead via CAPI when possible.

### Speed to lead

Contact within **5 minutes** (ideal: 60 seconds). Use SMS automation + closer alert.  
Source: [Hormozi — Close More Leads](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TI1Cltxl0fg).

### Creative angles (starter hooks)

## 9. VSL Strategy (Ads + Pre-Call)

Hormozi uses VSLs in two places: **after opt-in** and **after call booked** (warms buyer, cuts no-shows).  
Source: [VSL playbook](https://stormy.ai/blog/high-ticket-vsl-sales-funnel-playbook), [How to create a VSL](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bsPbiLTIaSo).

### VSL A — Post-opt-in (5–8 min) · "Why Booked Jobs Launch System"

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| 1. Identify | "If you own a local service business in Ontario…" |
| 2. Problem | Losing jobs to competitors on Google Maps; slow follow-up |
| 3. Mechanism | Website + GMB + missed-call text-back + reviews |
| 4. Proof | ShowRoom + DetailOps screenshots (30 sec each) |
| 5. Offer | What's included in $2,997 stack |
| 6. Guarantee | 14-day launch |
| 7. CTA | "Book your free strategy call — only open slots this week" |

**3-day rule:** Only show Calendly slots within **next 72 hours**.  
Source: [Stormy VSL playbook](https://stormy.ai/blog/high-ticket-vsl-sales-funnel-playbook).

### VSL B — Pre-sales-call (3–5 min) · "Before We Meet"

Send automatically when Calendly books:

- "Here's exactly what we'll cover on our call"  
- FAQ: timeline, what you need to prepare, what happens after yes  
- Reinforce guarantee + quick client win story  
- "Add this to your calendar" + reminder to watch before call  

**Goal:** Prospect arrives pre-sold on mechanism; call = fit check + close.

### VSL C — Retargeting ad cut (60–90 sec)

- Hook + one proof + CTA from VSL A  
- Use as Meta retargeting creative for LP visitors

### Production plan (low budget)

- Founder on camera OR screen recording + voiceover  
- CapCut edit · Teleprompter · Record in 1 session  
- Host: Mux (you already use on acquisition.com-style pages) or unlisted YouTube/Vimeo embed

## 10. Email Sequences & Onboarding

### Sequence 1 — Post-lead (didn't book) · 6 days

| Day | Subject angle | Content |
|-----|---------------|---------|
| 0 | Instant | VSL link + audit promise |
| 1 | "Did you see this?" | Link to VSL / GMB audit |
| 2 | One insight | "Why Google Maps beats your website for local trades" |
| 3 | Case study | ShowRoom or demo-site before/after |
| 4 | Objection | "I already have a website" |
| 5 | Scarcity | "3 Founding Client spots left this month" |
| 6 | Direct | "Book your call — here's the link" |

Source: [VSL email drip](https://stormy.ai/blog/high-ticket-vsl-sales-funnel-playbook).

### Sequence 2 — Pre-call (after book) · 3 emails

| Timing | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Immediate | Confirmation + **VSL B** + calendar link |
| 24 hr before | "Watch this before our call" + FAQ doc |
| 2 hr before | "We're on in 2 hours" + what to bring |

### Sequence 3 — Post-close onboarding · 14 days

| Day | Email | Action |
|-----|-------|--------|
| 0 | Welcome to the team | Onboarding link `/start/[token]` |
| 1 | Kickoff prep | Asset checklist |
| 3 | "We received your assets" | Set expectations |
| 7 | Draft preview | Website/GMB progress |
| 14 | Launch day | Go-live + review request link |
| 30 | Check-in | Upsell Grow tier |

**Hormozi principle:** Churn is a delivery problem. Quick win in Week 1 (GMB claimed + first review request).  
Source: [Churn is a delivery problem](https://usegavel.com/alex-hormozi/churn-is-a-delivery-problem).

### FAQ doc (attach to all sequences)

- What's included / not included  
- Timeline (14 days from payment + assets)  
- Who owns the website  
- Ongoing costs (hosting, Grow tier)  
- What we need from you (photos, logo, login access)

### Tools

- **Resend** (already in stack) or HubSpot if you want visual automation  
- Trigger from: form submit, Calendly webhook, Stripe paid invoice  
- n8n for glue (you already use for meeting booked)

## 11. Retention, Upsells, Referrals & Money Model

### Hormozi Money Model target

Collect **2× CAC + COGS in first 30 days**.  
Source: [Money Model](https://usegavel.com/alex-hormozi/money-model).

| Offer | When | Price | Purpose |
|-------|------|-------|---------|
| Launch | Day 0 | $2,997 | Core + cover delivery |
| **Grow** | Day 30 | $497–997/mo | Continuity + fund VA |
| **Scale** | Month 3–6 | $5k–15k | CRM/booking ascension |
| Review add-on | Month 3 | $200/mo | Attach to different "wallet" |
| Google Ads mgmt | Month 6 | $500–1k/mo | Lead gen wallet |

### "Buy more of what they bought" — X without Y

| After Launch they have… | Next problem | Upsell |
|-------------------------|--------------|--------|
| Great website | No traffic | Grow tier + GMB posts |
| GMB live | No reviews | Review management |
| Reviews flowing | Can't answer leads fast | Missed-call / AI text-back |
| More leads | Can't track jobs | Scale CRM (DetailOps-style) |
| Busy owner | No time for marketing | Done-for-you Meta ads mgmt |

**Sell at point of greatest deprivation** — when leads pick up and they're overwhelmed, not when they're "happy."  
Source: [Upsell playbook 2026](https://stormy.ai/blog/alex-hormozi-upsell-playbook-2026-ltv).

### Referral program

| Mechanism | Offer |
|-----------|-------|
| **Refer a trade owner** | $500 credit toward Grow OR free GMB audit for referral |
| **Two-sided** | Referrer + referee both get "Priority launch slot" |
| **Timing** | Ask at Day 30 check-in (after quick win delivered) |

Hormozi: incentivize enough to change behavior; best referrers = customers who got results.  
Source: [Customer referrals — $100M Leads](https://askthepods.com/episode/part-8-customer-referrals-100m-leads-book).

### Retention mechanics

- Monthly lead report (proves ROI)  
- Quarterly "GMB health check" call for Grow clients  
- Founding Client community (optional Slack/WhatsApp — curate, don't babysit)  
- **Referral to other owners they know** = easy value-add ("we'll audit your friend's GMB free")

### Optional front-end ascension (later)

**Local Visibility Sprint** — $297, 2 weeks, GMB audit + 5 quick wins → upsell to full Launch.  
Gym Launch "paid challenge" model. Source: [Money Model — front-load cash](https://usegavel.com/alex-hormozi/money-model).
