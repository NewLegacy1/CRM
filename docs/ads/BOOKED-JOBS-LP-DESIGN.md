# Booked Jobs LP — design plan

**Locked August 22, 2026.** Ads page only. Main site stays dark. Do not copy Acquisition.com yellow-on-black; take the **framework**, not the costume.

## Calendly

**Do not use it on this funnel.** Volume is founder-led. D5 is a callback in under 30 minutes. A calendar widget is a second decision after they already filled the form, and theirs is currently unavailable. Thank-you = “we’re calling you” + VSL A. `/book` can keep Calendly for the main site.

## Framework (Hormozi LP, not SaaS)

1. **First viewport:** money headline + the form. No scroll to convert.
2. **One offer.** No price on the page.
3. **Proof after the ask** — named shops, not fake $ figures.
4. **Mechanism** — 14 days, they close.
5. **Who it’s for / not for.**
6. **Repeat the same CTA.** No second offer.

## Why not the voicemail line

“If they hit your voicemail they booked the next guy” is a symptom. The sale is **lost money**. Default H1:

> You’re losing money every week to whoever answers first.

CTA: **Show me where I’m losing money**

## Visual system (less generic)

| Token | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F6F1E8` | Warm shop-ticket, not sterile white or galaxy black |
| Ink | `#16120E` | High contrast, readable at truck-distance type |
| Gold | `#E3A008` | One punch color for the button only |
| Rust | `#9A3412` | Money/emphasis — not ACQ yellow-only, not neon |
| Card | `#FFFCF7` | Form sits on paper like a work order |

- **Display:** Instrument Serif, sentence case. Kill the global uppercase.
- **UI:** Figtree. Big labels. 56px primary button.
- **Motion:** one 400ms fade-up on the hero. No glow, no galaxy, no bounce loops.
- **Form:** white card, hard ink border, gold submit. Qualifier stays; it is a filter, not a price tag.
