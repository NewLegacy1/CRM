# Transcript — Alex Hormozi's Lead Gen Strategy But Make It AI

**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6dYgYah3Ig  
**Channel:** Ryan Mathews  
**Pulled:** August 22, 2026 (YouTube captions, cleaned)

Hormozi clip (quoted): lead capture should use a mini-offer — complete solution to a narrow problem, lower cost or free — that reveals a second problem solved by the core offer. People who take the cheap/free thing are more likely to buy the related higher-cost thing later.

Ryan: traditional “book a call / request a quote” is asking for marriage on the first date. Works on the 1% who already trust you. Expensive leads. Hormozi’s “salty pretzel”: sell pretzels so more of the crowd gets thirsty, then sell water. Reciprocity (Cialdini). He cites Hormozi expecting 300–500% more lead volume from a better front-end (sometimes 10× vs a bad one). Example math: 1% → 3–5% opt-in.

His build: prospect requests an audit → generated in ~5 minutes → long structured report (above-the-fold, headline, CTA, social proof, structure, trust, UX, strengths, prioritized roadmap) → CTA: ideas don’t fix funnels; let us execute.

Stack: HighLevel page with a Vercel iframe form → Vercel → Claude with skill files (CRO rubric + how to fetch the site, including hosts that block naive scrapes) → consistent HTML/PDF template → writes back to HighLevel: tag `audit ready` + report URL → HighLevel email.

Thank-you page (while 5 min generate): “Before you check your email, watch this about your audit.” Video: good ideas + poor execution = zero. Calendar under the video. “Check email in about 5 minutes.” Hormozi: give information free, sell implementation.

First email: here’s your audit (light ask). Then ~5 more emails: have you done anything, case results, book the calendar. Modeled on Hormozi’s Scaling Roadmap nurture → Vegas workshop.

Mentions Karpathy “auto research” for headline/CTA tests. Ryan has not used it. Fast-feedback only; bad when tests take 2–4 weeks. Skip at low traffic.

Cost: ~10–30 cents per report vs hundreds for a human. 80% quality at ~0.1% cost.

CTA: his waitlist to build this for you.
