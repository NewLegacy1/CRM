## 1. What New Legacy AI actually is

**One company, two tracks** — same team, same tech stack, different buyers and landing pages.

| | **Track A — Local Growth** | **Track B — Custom Software** |
|--|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Buyer** | Owner-operators, local service businesses | Firms with budget ($500k–$50M+ revenue), ops pain |
| **Offer** | Website + GMB + lead **capture** automations | CRM, booking, internal tools, AI workflows |
| **Ticket** | $1k–3k launch + $500–1k/mo grow | $5k–$50k+ projects + retainers |
| **Geography** | Canada + USA (remote delivery) | Canada + USA |
| **Primary channel** | Meta ads + cold call → **siloed LP** | Outbound, referrals, main site, case studies |
| **Proof** | ShowRoom, Jay, DetailOps (detailer angle) | DetailOps platform, CRM builds, funnel analytics |

**You are not “a trades agency.”** You are a **remote digital systems company** with a **productized entry offer** for local businesses and a **high-ticket build practice** for everyone else.

## 4. Track A — Local Growth (trades & home services)

### Why a siloed landing page

- Meta ads need **one avatar, one offer, one message** (Hormozi).
- Main site stays **broad**; trades LP is **narrow** for conversion only.
- **Do not link trades LP from main nav** — only ads, cold email, cold call follow-up.

**URL (proposed):** `newlegacyai.ca/booked-jobs` or `get.newlegacyai.ca`  
**Robots:** optional `noindex` if you want zero organic overlap (your choice).

### Trades LP content (single page)

1. Hook: *Stop losing jobs on Google Maps*  
2. Who it’s for: trades & home services, Canada/USA, 2+ years in business  
3. Problem: invisible / untrusted / voicemail  
4. Offer: **Booked Jobs Launch** from $2,497 (GMB + site + capture automations)  
5. Optional: **Visibility Fix** from $997  
6. Proof: ShowRoom + Jay (+ DetailOps as “we build booking systems”)  
7. FAQ + qualify form or Calendly  
8. VSL (5–8 min) post-opt-in  

### Track A packages

| Package | Price (CAD) | Includes |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Visibility Fix | $997–1,497 | GMB, reviews, simple site or GMB-first |
| Booked Jobs Launch | $2,497–2,997 | Above + conversion site + missed-call text-back + alerts + 14-day launch |
| Grow | $497–997/mo | GMB posts, reviews, monthly lead report |
| Respond *(future)* | $800–1,500/mo | VA/AI first response — **only when you can deliver** |

### Track A marketing

- **Meta:** $100/day Rule of 100 → trades LP only  
- **Cold call:** demo sites → book → Launch or upsell Scale later  
- **Main site:** case studies + “Local business systems” section — not the primary ad destination

## 5. Track B — Custom software (higher end)

### Buyer

- Multi-location or 10+ employees  
- Outgrown spreadsheets, Jobber/Housecall Pro/Shopify admin hell  
- Budget for **$5k–$50k** builds  

### Offer

- Custom CRM, booking, client portals, automations, AI assistants  
- **DetailOps** as proof you ship real product  
- ShowRoom funnel as proof you understand conversion  

### Track B marketing

- **Main site** hero + “Custom systems” section + `/crm-intake`  
- **No Meta broad ads** at first — outbound, LinkedIn, referrals, partnerships  
- Cold call **can** pitch Scale when prospect has team + pain (not one-man band)  
- Hormozi: cold outreach economics favor **$10k+** tickets  

### Track B packages (indicative)

| Tier | Range | Examples |
|------|-------|----------|
| Discovery + roadmap | $1,500–3,000 | Audit, workflow map, fixed SOW |
| Build | $5k–25k | CRM, booking, integrations |
| Retainer | $1k–5k/mo | Iteration, support, ops |

## 6. Main website changes (stay open, not trades-only)

**Goal:** Credible for **both** tracks. Trades depth lives on siloed LP.

### Hero (3 beats — replace AI/sci-fi copy)

1. **Systems that turn attention into revenue** — local or scaling firms, Canada & USA  
2. **Local businesses** — Google, website, lead capture (link to trades LP or #local)  
3. **Growing firms** — custom CRM, booking, automation (link to #custom or crm-intake)  

### Homepage sections (order)

1. Hero  
2. **Two paths** — cards: “Local growth” | “Custom software”  
3. How we work (remote, 14-day local / phased builds for software)  
4. Case studies (all three — trades + product)  
5. Industries we serve (broader list OK — but two paths clarify who clicks where)  
6. FAQ  
7. CTA: Book call / Get in touch  

### Remove / soften on main site

- “Universe of AI” / “Explore the frontier”  
- Six equal AI service cards → **two paths** + optional “More capabilities” link  
- Implying you answer leads for clients  

### Keep on main site

- Galaxy visual brand  
- All case studies  
- Industries page (position as “we adapt the playbook”)  
- Calendly, lead form, crm-intake, site-intake  

### Nav (proposed)

- **Local systems** → `/#local` or external trades LP  
- **Custom software** → `/#custom` or `/crm-intake`  
- **Work** → case studies  
- **Book a call**  

**Do not add** trades LP to nav.
