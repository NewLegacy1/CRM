# Sales call SOP — Booked Jobs Launch

They should have watched **VSL B** and texted **READY**.  
Call length: ~15 min if they did. ~20 if they watch B on the call.

---

## Before you join

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **VSL confirmed** | They texted READY after VSL B |
| Missing | Booked, didn’t watch the pre-call video |

Open: their audit PDF, their Google listing, a notes line for the five facts.

---

## No READY text

Do not skip. Do not say this in a video — only live, calm.

> “I send a short video before these so we don’t do discovery on the call. I don’t have your text yet. I’m dropping the link. Watch it, text READY, I’ll grab a coffee and come back.”

Send **VSL B** (not A). Wait. Then start below.

If they refuse: reschedule. No free consulting call.

---

## They texted READY

Skip coffee. Do **not** re-do discovery.

1. **Confirm the leak (3–4 min)** — “Which of the three was it — can’t find you, they skip you, or you miss the call?” Pull Maps. Read two numbers off their audit. That’s the diagnose.
2. **Confirm the five (2 min)** — jobs/month, who answers, cities, avg ticket, GMB yes/no. They should already have these. Fill gaps only.
3. **Fit (1 min)** — owner on the call, 2+ years, can invest.
4. **Offer (4–5 min)** — Launch. $2,997 PIF. 14 days. Capture, they close. If they can’t: Fix, then payment plan. Not a menu.
5. **Close** — invoice same day.

---

## After

Paid → Seq 3. Not paid → 24h follow-up. No-show → one SMS, one email, one reschedule.
