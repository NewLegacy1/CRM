# Hormozi Framework Critique Checklist — New Legacy AI

**For master agent stress-testing.** Score each item Pass / Fail / Gap with evidence and a fix.

**Primary sources:** [$100M Offers](https://shop.acquisition.com/pages/100m-offers) · [$100M Leads](https://www.acquisition.com/roadmap) · [Ep 999 service business](https://podbrain.app/notes/alex-hormozi/if-i-wanted-to-scale-a-service-business-in-2026-heres-what-id-do-ep-999) · [Ep 898 funnel](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2025-06-02-episode-summary-the-funnel-we-use-to-make-250m-ep-898) · [2026 cold economics](https://daniliants.com/insights/alex-hormozi-2026-ai-agencies-cold-email/) · [Rule of 100](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XrR6TGMfIOU) · [Stage 3 Stabilize](https://www.acquisition.com/training/stabilize)

## A. $100M Offers

1. **Dream outcome headline** — Sell "booked jobs / leads reach you fast," not "website + GMB + AI."
2. **Crush time + effort** — 14-day done-for-you launch explicit.
3. **Proof stack** — ShowRoom, DetailOps, case studies before price.
4. **Trim & stack** — One default offer; downsells on call only; no choice paralysis.
5. **Price virtuous cycle** — $2,497–2,997 PIF; payment plans cost more than PIF.

## B. $100M Leads

6. **One Core Four channel for 90 days (Track A)** — Meta → siloed LP primary; cold secondary only.
7. **Rule of 100** — $100 CAD/day Meta × 100 days with 20+ pre-built hooks.
8. **Lead magnet** — GMB audit / visibility report (obviously valuable), not bare Calendly.
9. **Cold economics** — Cold outreach primary only for $10k+ Track B; not $2.5k Launch.
10. **MVP organic** — 3 posts/week on one channel if any outbound runs (profile social proof).

## C. $100M Money Models

11. **30-day cash** — GP in 30 days > 2×(CAC + COGS); include amortized Meta CAC.
12. **Offer sequence** — Launch (attraction) → Grow (continuity) → Scale (ascension); scripted at Day 14.
13. **Payment plans** — Minimum cash before work starts (layaway logic).

## D. VSL / Sales

14. **Funnel order** — Magnet → VSL A → qualify → book → VSL B → call.
15. **Intentional friction** — Form filters avatar; qualified > volume.
16. **Speed to lead** — Target <60 sec; minimum <5 min automated.
17. **Documented sales SOP** — Script, objections, plus-plus/minus-minus before hiring closer.

## E. Ep 999 — Service Business 2026

18. **Trim delivery before scaling ads** — Templates + VA; no bespoke Launch builds.
19. **Avatar quality** — 2+ years, established owner-operators; not any local business.

## F. Stage 3 Stabilize

20. **Stage fit** — Document → hire VA → one channel → quality; not hire closer before SOPs.
21. **VA timing** — After 2 Launch/mo + checklists exist; Grow MRR covers VA cost.

## G. Anti-patterns (sub-$10k)

22. Cold as primary Track A engine · Multi-avatar Meta at small budget · Promise lead answering without Respond · Meta to main site · Seven industries one ad set.

## H. Andromeda / Meta

23. **Creative = targeting** — One avatar, broad geo, 10–50 hook variants.
24. **Horizontal scaling** — Win hook in Ontario first → then new trade/state.
25. **LP > lead form** — Cold traffic to siloed LP + VSL for $2.5k+ offer.

## Scoring rubric

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0–8 Pass | Aligned |
| 9–15 Gap | Fix sequencing, math, or docs |
| 16+ Fail | Structural mismatch |

## Known high-risk gaps in current plan

1. Track A cold call at $2.5k vs $10k+ cold economics  
2. Meta + cold in parallel vs one channel 90 days  
3. 5-min speed-to-lead vs 60-sec system  
4. Two-track brand vs one avatar/offer/LP for ads  
5. 30-day cash must include Meta CAC amortization
